Why Urbman

Urbman started with a simple idea: a man should not need a big-city mall to find a shirt that fits and holds its colour.

We keep the range small and check every piece ourselves before it reaches the rack. If it is not right, we do not sell it.

  • Menswear only, so every piece is chosen with one customer in mind
  • Size help on WhatsApp before you spend anything
  • Fabric details shared openly: cotton blend, GSM, wash
  • Phone is answered by the shop, not a call centre
  • Small range, restocked often
